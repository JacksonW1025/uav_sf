# Stage A2 remediation preregistration

This is a new formal study, not an extension or re-evaluation of the closed
primary Stage A2 ledger. The primary 51-launch denominator and every retained
outcome remain unchanged.

The remediation changes exactly two infrastructure contracts. First, a
command-consumption observation whose subject predates the clock bridge is
omitted as temporally unbound, matching the existing rule for downstream
stages; later bound evidence is still required. Second, both mechanisms must
complete public Takeoff, enter `internal_hold`, and hold it for the frozen dwell
before requesting the tested route. The Evidence Gate independently requires
physical takeoff to precede the tested transition.

All workload geometry, observer profile, Oracle thresholds, safety limits,
physical metrics, target sizes, launch caps, four-slot execution resources,
and paired-block structure are unchanged. New seed ranges, image, environment
identity, attempt prefixes, ledger, and denominator prevent mixing with the
primary study.

Normal cells target five accepted traces with a cap of ten; stall cells target
eight with a cap of sixteen. A null or violating result is retained. Claims
remain bounded to the attested Thor SITL task in paper Section 6.
